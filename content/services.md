At OurEmbeddeds, we deliver comprehensive solutions for embedded, industrial, and IoT projects. Our expertise covers the full product lifecycle, from concept to deployment and support.

## Embedded Systems & Firmware
Robust, high-quality software for mission-critical and industrial applications.
- **Mission-critical firmware:** Aerospace-grade, safety-focused development for demanding environments.
- **Microcontroller firmware:** Baremetal, RTOS, and safety systems for all major architectures.
- **Embedded Linux:** Custom distributions with Yocto, Buildroot, or mainstream distros for advanced devices.
- **System software & backend:** Integration of embedded devices with cloud and enterprise systems.
- **FPGA + Firmware co-design:** Joint hardware/software solutions for performance and flexibility.
- **Softcores & RISC-V:** Custom processor architectures and open hardware innovation.

## Hardware & Electronics
From schematic to production, we turn ideas into reliable products.
- **Electronic hardware design:** Schematics, simulation, and prototyping for new devices.
- **PCB design & manufacturing:** High-speed, multi-layer, and production-ready boards.
- **RF & antenna engineering:** Design and validation for wireless, IoT, and aerospace applications.
